## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, Michelin-inspired culinary presence tailored for Mr Dimsum. It bridges heritage Cantonese dim sum craftsmanship with a contemporary, high-end editorial dining aesthetic. The visual tone is warm, deliberate, and lavish yet minimalist, stripping away decorative clutter to highlight culinary mastery, pristine ingredient sourcing, and hospitality.

The design philosophy combines high-end editorial minimalism with warm tactile nuances:
- **Atmosphere:** Sunlit bamboo steam baskets, roasted amber teas, and clean ceramic table settings.
- **Editorial Poise:** Anchored by high-contrast serif typography paired with hyper-legible contemporary geometric sans-serif body copy.
- **Spatial Generosity:** Wide margins, breathing room around tasting courses, and deliberate grid framing evoke unhurried luxury dining.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in caramelized sugars, toasted sesame, bamboo parchment, and steeped black tea:

- **Primary (`#CC6F00`):** Rich caramel/terracotta. Acts as the foundational anchor for key actions, brand seals, active states, and focal accents.
- **Secondary (`#F2A900`):** Luminous amber gold. Used for subtle highlights, badges, ratings, and culinary emphasis.
- **Tertiary (`#F9E6A8`):** Steamed wheat and custard tone. Functions as a gentle wash for tinted badges, subtle dividers, and high-priority callout backgrounds.
- **Neutral (`#4D2A00`):** Deep roasted espresso. Replaces harsh blacks to deliver rich, high-contrast typography, structural outlines, and dignified editorial headlines.
- **Surfaces (`#FDFBF7`, `#F5EFEB`, `#ECE3DC`, `#FFFFFF`):** Warm parchment and ceramic tones provide stacked background contrast without visual sterility.

## Typography

The pairing of Playfair Display and Plus Jakarta Sans crafts a balance between classical gastronomic tradition and clean modern interaction:

- **Playfair Display:** Carries emotional weight, grace, and culinary prestige across all prominent headings, tasting notes, and section titles.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Provides welcoming clarity, generous x-height, and open letterforms for long descriptions, dish ingredients, nutritional indices, and functional UI elements.
- **Label Tokens:** Styled with purposeful letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) and uppercase treatment to evoke artisanal menu classification headers.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid underpins the layout, with responsive bounds maxing out at `1280px` to maintain focused editorial scanning.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with `2rem` (`32px`) gutters and `5rem` (`80px`) exterior margins. Sections breathe with `space-2xl` to `space-3xl` vertical padding.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `3rem` (`48px`) margins. Multi-dish presentation grids condense gracefully from 3 columns to 2.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.5rem` (`24px`) outer margin. Menus switch from dual-column editorial layout into seamless vertical flows.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows deliberate intervals where related culinary groupings sit within `space-sm` to `space-md`, while thematic course separations leverage `space-xl` and above.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than synthetic gray dropshadows, the design system utilizes warm ambient lighting reminiscent of softly backlit dining dining rooms and layered stoneware:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base canvas: `#FDFBF7` (warm cream).
  - Raised cards & plates: `#FFFFFF` (crisp ceramic white).
  - Recessed compartments & trays: `#F5EFEB` (honed dough tone).
- **Ambient Warm Shadows:**
  - **Level 1 (Hover / Subdued Cards):** `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(77, 42, 0, 0.05), 0px 1px 3px rgba(77, 42, 0, 0.03)`
  - **Level 2 (Active Plates & Menus):** `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(77, 42, 0, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(77, 42, 0, 0.04)`
  - **Level 3 (Modals & Flyouts):** `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(77, 42, 0, 0.12), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(77, 42, 0, 0.04)`
- **Linework:** Subtle ghost boundaries using `1px solid rgba(77, 42, 0, 0.08)` to frame ceramic surfaces without visual heaviness.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a roundedness factor of `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline radius), capturing the gentle curvature of steam baskets, polished tableware, and porcelain spoons without drifting into juvenile bubbly forms:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used on interactive controls, inputs, dropdown menus, and micro badges.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / `16px`):** Applied to standard presentation cards, photo frames, and reservation modals.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / `24px`):** Dedicated to primary dish hero features, degustation menu containers, and prominent banner cards.
- **Pill Shape:** Reserved strictly for tags, status indicators, and specialized icon buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Reserve a Table", "Order Tasting Set"):** Background `#CC6F00`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Hover transitions to `#A65800` with subtle elevation Level 1.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Explore Story"):** Transparent fill with `1.5px` border in `#4D2A00`, text `#4D2A00`. Hover fills with `#F5EFEB`.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Text `#CC6F00` with animated bottom underline.

### Cards & Menu Showcases
- **Dish Feature Card:** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), border `1px solid rgba(77, 42, 0, 0.08)`, border-radius `1rem`. Generous inner padding (`1.5rem`), featuring high-resolution photography cropped with `0.75rem` internal radius. Title set in Playfair Display with pricing in bold Plus Jakarta Sans (`#4D2A00`).
- **Chef's Recommendation Accent Card:** Tinted background (`#FDFBF7`) with top border accent (`3px solid #CC6F00`) and warm Level 2 shadow.

### Chips & Badges
- **Dietary / Category Chips (e.g., "Handmade Daily", "Gluten-Free"):** Pill-shaped, background `#F9E6A8` with `30%` opacity, border `1px solid rgba(204, 111, 0, 0.2)`, text `#4D2A00`, font `label-sm`.
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** Default on `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid rgba(77, 42, 0, 0.12)`. When selected, fills `#CC6F00` with white text.

### Form Inputs
- **Reservation Fields & Selectors:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(77, 42, 0, 0.15)`, text `#4D2A00`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.875rem 1rem`. Focus state triggers border `#CC6F00` and `0 0 0 3px rgba(204, 111, 0, 0.15)`.

### Lists & Degustation Index
- **Menu Index Item:** Horizontal flex structure. Left side holds the course title in Playfair Display and ingredients in Plus Jakarta Sans (`body-sm`). Dotted botanical leader line connects to right-aligned price in `#CC6F00`. Separators utilize `1px solid rgba(77, 42, 0, 0.06)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** `20px` square or circle with `1.5px solid rgba(77, 42, 0, 0.25)`. Checked state fills `#CC6F00` displaying a clean white icon checkmark.
